🎓 An Educational Web Search Engine

A search engine dedicated exclusively to learning and educational knowledge, covering users from school students to researchers.

The interesting part is that we're not limiting it to one subject. We're limiting it by purpose: learning/education.

The scope could be:
User group	Examples
🧒 School	Mathematics, physics, chemistry, history, geography
🎓 College	Engineering, commerce, science, arts, law, etc.
💻 Technology	Programming, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, databases
📊 Management	MBA, finance, marketing, HR, operations
🧬 Medical	Medicine, biology, anatomy, pharmacology
🔬 Research	Papers, journals, preprints, technical articles
📚 General learning	Tutorials, documentation, educational blogs
🛠️ Professional	AWS, Python, Git, Docker, certifications
📖 Reference	Encyclopedic/reference educational material

So the product isn't:

"Search the entire internet."

It's:

"Search the educational internet."

But I would make one important change

I wouldn't call the project simply:

"Educational Search Engine"

I'd position it as:

EduSearch — A Specialized Educational Web Search Engine

or potentially a more distinctive name later.

Find reliable learning resources, not the entire internet.

But there is one major challenge

We should not promise that every educational resource on the internet will be available.

That's technically unrealistic.

There are:

billions of web pages
paywalls
robots.txt restrictions
private course platforms
dynamically generated content
copyrighted material
websites that block crawlers
rapidly changing content

So our project description should say something like:

A specialized search engine for discovering publicly accessible educational resources across academic, technical, professional, and research domains.

That's technically defensible.
